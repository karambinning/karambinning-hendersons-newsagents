The papers, the lottery, the bottle of pop you came in for — all on the way home.

Hendersons is the kind of newsagent that knows its postcode by heart. It sits where Hitchin Road bends north out of Round Green, a few doors from the bus stop, with the day’s broadsheets and tabloids stacked by the door and the magazine rack along the side wall.

Pop in for the morning paper, a top-up for the mobile, a scratchcard on the way past, or one of the colder soft drinks at the back of the chiller. Cards, sweets, tobacco, and the small basics that make the walk home a little shorter — it’s all here, under one shop sign, on the corner of LU2.

No reservations, no app, no loyalty card to forget. Just step in, ask for what you need, and you’ll be back out on the pavement in under a minute.

Round Green · Luton

Step in off Hitchin Road. Mind the door.

Hendersons is on the east side of Hitchin Road as it climbs out of town, between the residential terraces of Round Green. There is on-street parking nearby and bus stops in both directions a short walk away.

Hours are not posted online — if you’re travelling specially, please ring the shop before setting off.
